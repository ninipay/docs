<Tip>
**Example:** Gia says there are no unmatched records. You still need to check the Matches waiting on review: a grouped item can have missing bank evidence or a delivery issue.

<Info>
**Before you start:** Keep the Gia response available and note its organization, dates and source names.
</Info>

## How Gia shows its evidence

- **Numbered markers:** A small number in brackets after a statement points to its source. Hover over it to preview the source, and click it to open the record, document or Match once the answer has finished.
- **Sources list:** Expand Sources under the answer to see every cited source with its type. A source that stands for a group of transactions opens a panel listing those transactions.
- **Transactions beside the answer:** When Gia lays out transactions, each row shows the payee, date, amount and whether it is already matched, with a total when there are several. Select a row to open the record. A statement check compares the statement's figures with its lines and shows whether they add up or how far off they are.
- **Sources panel:** On a wide screen, the panel beside the conversation repeats the cited sources and, when the conversation is about a month, shows that month's open items and what is blocking its export.

## Step by step

1. Read the exact claim. Identify whether it concerns one transaction, a list of examples, a total or the completion of an entire period.
2. Open the numbered source or the transaction row beside the relevant statement. Check that the destination is the record or transaction the answer describes.
3. Compare the source date, amount, currency, counterparty and money direction. Open the original document when the question depends on an invoice or statement.
4. For completion claims, open Records, switch to the Matches tab and inspect the Matches still waiting on review for the same period. Also check Questions for the month's open items, Documents for validation work and Books for ledger delivery where relevant.
5. If the source and the answer disagree, ask Gia to explain that specific discrepancy and show its sources. Keep the unresolved item on your review list.
6. Base the next action on the underlying evidence and your review process. A response saying "done" is not a substitute for checking the actual state of the item.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You can identify which evidence supports the answer and which claims still need checking.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If a citation is missing, inaccessible or unrelated, ask for another source and inspect the relevant area directly. Do not infer that missing evidence proves a payment did not occur.
</Warning>
