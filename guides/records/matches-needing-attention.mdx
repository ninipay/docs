---
title: "Find Matches that need attention"
description: "A Match brings together records believed to describe the same financial event."
---

The Matches view helps you find missing evidence, conflicting information or a delivery issue that needs investigation.

<Tip>
**Example:** An invoice and receipt are grouped together but no bank payment is attached. The reason No bank transaction yet tells you what evidence is missing.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Have the period you want to review. Needs you, Ready to approve and In your books are groups to inspect, not a single guarantee that all checks passed.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Records in the sidebar.
2. Click Matches in the view switch beside Records.
3. Check the date range and Filters. Filters narrow by source and tag; Ambiguous only shows Matches where another Match wants the same line.
4. Find the Needs you group. Read the reason under each item, such as No bank transaction yet, Money in at the bank, money out in the books, Nini suggested lines to check or Didn't reach your books.
5. Click Review on one row to open its Match page.
6. Follow [Inspect the evidence and status of a Match](/guides/records/inspect-a-match) to compare the evidence and delivery message. Use the next arrow on the Match page, or return to Matches, to inspect the next item.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You know why one Match needs attention and have opened the evidence behind that reason.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If no items appear, check dates and filters. If an In your books item has a warning, inspect it: posting and evidence quality are separate.
</Warning>

## The other groups

- **Ready to approve** holds Matches with nothing flagged. Click Approve to add one to your books, or open it first if you want to check the evidence.
- **In a closed month** holds Matches dated inside a month that has been closed. Reopen that month before approving them.
- **In your books** holds approved Matches. **Undone** holds Matches that were undone or reversed.
