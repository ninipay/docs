---
title: "Find and inspect individual records"
description: "Records brings together every transaction from your connected accounts and documents, and shows whether each one is in your books."
---

The views at the top of the list help you find review work; the filters narrow the wider list.

<Tip>
**Example:** You want to know which imported items still need someone to look at them. Start with To review for the period you are responsible for.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Choose the period and source you want to review. A record can already belong to a Match and still need attention.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Records in the sidebar. Check that Records, not Matches, is selected in the view switch.
2. Set the date range. The Where your transactions end up flow summarizes records in that range; click part of the flow to narrow the list to it.
3. Choose a view: To review for lines that are not matched or in your books yet, To approve for Matches waiting for approval, In your books, Ignored or All. Each row's status says what it needs, such as Needs a match, Check Nini's suggestion or Awaiting bank.
4. Use Filters to narrow by source, tag, account or amount. Active filters appear as chips above the list; clear them before concluding there is no work.
5. Click a row, or open its Row actions menu and choose Open, to read the record in a side panel. Choose Open full page for the full record with its details and history. Use J and K to move between records and Esc to close the panel.
6. Read the record's source, date, amount and any Match it belongs to. Use [Inspect the evidence and status of a Match](/guides/records/inspect-a-match) if grouped evidence appears.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You have opened a specific item for review and know the period, view and filters used to find it.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If the list is empty, check the date range, view and filters before concluding there is no work. Compare counts only when dates, filters and the type of item counted match.
</Warning>

## Act on several records

Select rows with their checkboxes to open the selection bar. Match groups the selected records into a Match. Mark as in your books, Ignore and Remove change a record's state; use them only when your review process calls for that decision. Ignored records stay in the Ignored view, where Reopen brings them back.

<Note>
When Gia has questions about the lines in To review, a Gia has questions about these lines button appears above the list. Click it to answer them, then use Back to To review to return.
</Note>
