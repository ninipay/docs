---
title: "Find your way around Nini"
description: "The sidebar is your starting point."
---

A tab changes the view inside an area, and a filter limits which items are shown. Knowing the difference makes it easier to find an item without changing it.

<Tip>
**Example:** To inspect an original invoice, start in Documents. To see the invoice together with a bank payment, open Records and choose the Matches tab.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** A path such as Records → Matches means click Records in the sidebar, then the Matches tab. A drawer is a panel that opens beside the list.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Find the left sidebar. Its destinations are grouped under headings: Insights holds Ask and Questions for working with Gia; Ledger holds Accounts for bank information and Books for your accounting system; Reconcile holds Documents for files and Records for review work. Integrations and Settings sit under Workspace at the bottom.
2. Click the area you need. Read the page heading and the breadcrumb at the top of the page to confirm where you are before using its controls.
3. In Records, use the tabs at the top to choose Records for individual transactions or Matches for grouped evidence. A Sets tab appears once your organization has sets of related events.
4. In the Records tab, choose a view such as To review, To approve, In your books, Ignored or All. A view changes which items are listed, not the items themselves.
5. Check any date, status, source or other filter controls. A filter can hide an item that exists.
6. To find something without knowing where it lives, use the search box at the top of the page, or press Cmd+K (Ctrl+K on Windows).
7. Use the sidebar to move to another area. Use a drawer's close button or the back arrow in the top bar to return to the list behind it. The button beside the breadcrumb collapses the sidebar to icons when you need more room.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You can open the right area and recognize when a tab, view or filter changes what you see.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If a menu is absent, ask your administrator about access and feature availability. Some areas appear only for certain roles or once they are turned on for your organization. A missing menu does not mean your data was deleted.
</Warning>
