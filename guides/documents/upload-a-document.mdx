---
title: "Upload a document to the right destination"
description: "Uploading brings a file into Nini."
---

The destination tells Nini whether the file goes into your books, after you check it, or is only for Gia to read and learn about your business.

<Tip>
**Example:** A supplier invoice to compare with a payment belongs in For your books. A background report you want Gia to read belongs in For Gia to read.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Have the correct file ready and check whether it is already in Documents. Supported formats are PDF, CSV, XLSX, JPG, PNG, BAI2, TXT and XML, up to 50 MB per file.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Documents in the sidebar, then click Upload.
2. Choose where the files go at the top of the dialog. For your books goes into your books once you check it; For Gia to read stays out of your books.
3. Click Choose files and select one or more files, or drag them into the dialog. You can also drag files straight onto the Documents page.
4. Check the files listed under Ready to upload and the destination you chose. Remove any wrong file with its X, then click the upload button, which shows how many files you are adding.
5. Watch the Uploads panel on the Documents page. When an upload finishes, Nini reads the file; it shows as Reading until it is ready.
6. Open the file and check its contents using [Review information extracted from a document](/guides/documents/review-extracted-information). A successful upload is not the same as a completed review.

<Check>
**Expected result:** The file appears in Documents under the destination you chose and can be opened.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** Files over 50 MB are not added; the dialog names them. If an upload fails, use Try again in the Uploads panel. If Nini recognizes a file it already has, the copy shows as Already uploaded; do not keep uploading the same file.
</Warning>

<Note>
If you chose the wrong destination, right-click the document in the list and choose the other destination. You can also email files to the address shown at the bottom of the Upload dialog, or bring them in from a connected folder with Cloud drives. See [Route emailed documents](/guides/documents/email-intake).
</Note>
