It helps you find an item, see the evidence behind it and identify work that still needs attention. You do not need to understand every menu before starting.

<Tip>
**Example:** You want to check whether a supplier payment has an invoice attached. First confirm the organization, then find the payment and inspect its evidence.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Have the Nini link and sign-in account provided by your organization. This guide starts after you have access; invitation and sign-in recovery are separate processes.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Open the Nini link supplied by your organization. On the Sign in screen, enter your work email and click Continue, then finish signing in with your assigned account. If your organization uses Google accounts, you may see Continue with Google instead.
2. If you belong to more than one organization, Nini may show Choose a workspace. Pick the workspace you want to enter. If your account has a default organization, Nini opens it directly.
3. Read the organization name under your name at the bottom of the left sidebar. This identifies the workspace whose information you are viewing.
4. To change organization, click your name at the bottom of the sidebar. Under Switch organization, select the one you need; the current one has a check mark. Choose All workspaces to return to the full workspace list.
5. Check the name at the bottom of the sidebar again before uploading, connecting a source or reviewing records. If the organization is not listed, ask your administrator to check your membership.
6. Use the sidebar to open the area you need. Start with [Find your way around Nini](/guides/navigation) to learn the navigation. If you already have connected data, try the guided review in [Review your work from start to finish](/guides/review/end-to-end-review).
7. When you are finished, open the same account menu and choose Sign out, especially on a shared computer.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You know which organization you are in and which task to start with.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If you cannot sign in, or Nini shows No organization access, contact the person who gave you access. Reconnecting a bank is not a sign-in fix.
</Warning>
