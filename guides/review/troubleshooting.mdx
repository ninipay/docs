This often explains missing items or different counts and gives your support contact enough information to investigate.

<Tip>
**Example:** A statement looks missing because Documents is showing For Gia to read while the file was uploaded For your books.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Keep the relevant screen open. Note what you expected to see and what actually appeared.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Confirm the organization under your name at the bottom of the sidebar and the area named in the page heading and breadcrumb.
2. Check the date range and active filters. For Documents, also check whether you are viewing For your books or For Gia to read, and the status filter. For Records, check the selected view, such as To review or All. For Accounts, check the selected account and sync state.
3. Read the exact status or error. If a control is disabled, read the explanation shown beside it. For a Match, read evidence warnings and ledger delivery separately.
4. Open the original source or supporting record. Compare its date, amount, currency and description with the item in Nini.
5. Write down the steps that led to the problem, the expected result and the actual result. Include the period, filters and exact message. Copy your Organization ID from Settings → Details so support can find the right workspace.
6. Use your organization's agreed support contact if it remains unresolved. Share only the details needed to reproduce it; avoid repeated uploads, retries or reconnects.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You have either explained the difference or prepared a clear, reproducible question for support.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If Gia says work is complete, compare the live To review view and the Needs you section in Matches for the same period. If a Match says it is in your books but your accounting system has no new entry, open the Match's ledger delivery details and check Integrations for a connection that needs attention.
</Warning>
