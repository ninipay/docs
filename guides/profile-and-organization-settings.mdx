Use it to locate the right control and understand who should manage it.

<Tip>
**Example:** Changing your viewing preference is different from changing an organization detail that other users share.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Available sections depend on your role and what is enabled for your organization. Some fields save automatically, so inspect existing values without typing into them.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Settings at the bottom of the sidebar, then Profile under You. You can also choose Profile from the account menu under your name. Read the displayed name and email to confirm which account is signed in.
2. The Profile page says name and email are managed by your identity provider. Use your organization's sign-in administration process to correct them; this page is not an editable contact form.
3. Open Appearance for display choices. If Document intake is shown under You, use it to email or text documents to Nini and choose where they are filed; see [Route emailed documents](/guides/documents/email-intake).
4. Under Organization, open Details. Identity shows the organization name, the Organization ID and the reporting currency. Only an admin can rename the organization, and changes save automatically. Copy the Organization ID when a support contact asks for it.
5. Details also shows At a glance links to Members, Integrations and, when available, Plan & usage. Its Danger zone lets you leave the organization; an admin must remain, so the last admin cannot leave.
6. Use Integrations for connected sources. Members and Billing & usage appear only for admins; work through your organization's responsible administrator for those changes.
7. Developer access, under Organization, connects Gia, AI tools and your organization's software to Nini. Privacy, at the foot of the settings list, controls what Nini may record about how you use the app. You do not need developer setup for the everyday review workflows in this guide.
8. For automatic approval and how sure Nini must be before it matches transactions, open Matching under Organization, covered in [Read matching settings](/guides/records/matching-settings). These are organization settings, not personal preferences.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You know whether your question belongs to personal settings, organization administration or matching settings.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If a section is absent, confirm your role and feature availability with an administrator. Leaving an organization removes your access right away, and only an admin can add you back.
</Warning>
