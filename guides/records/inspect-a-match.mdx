---
title: "Inspect the evidence and status of a Match"
description: "The Match page lets you compare the bank evidence, the accounting or document evidence, and the ledger-delivery message."
---

Reading all three helps you identify the actual next problem to solve.

<Tip>
**Example:** A Match is approved but has no bank transaction and says Ledger delivery never requested. Approval alone does not mean evidence is complete or delivery happened.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Open Records, switch to Matches and click a row, or click Review on a row under Needs you. The Bank side holds bank evidence; the Books side holds the related invoices, bills, receipts or accounting records.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Read the amount, party and status at the top of the page, such as Ready to approve, Needs approval or In your books.
2. Read the Bank side. Identify the bank record, or note that it is missing.
3. Read each Books side record. Compare the party, date, amount, currency and money direction with the bank evidence. Equal amounts alone do not prove they belong together. Read Why Nini matched these for the reasons behind the suggestion.
4. Read any warning, such as a missing bank transaction or money in at the bank but money out in the books. Lines marked Suggested were added by Nini; Confirm each one or remove it from its line menu.
5. Read the ledger-delivery message separately. Distinguish In your books, Couldn't add to your books and Ledger delivery never requested; do not infer delivery from approval.
6. Use History and Details for more context. Use the previous and next arrows, or K and J, to move to the next Match without returning to the list.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You can state whether the problem is missing evidence, conflicting evidence, ledger delivery or more than one of these.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** Approve adds the Match to your books, and Retry posting sends it to the ledger again; neither merely reloads the page. Resolve the evidence and confirm the intended action before using them. To start over, use More actions → Undo match.
</Warning>

<Note>
Click the Gia icon to ask Gia about this Match, for example why there is no bank transaction yet. Nini ledger posting is separate from writing back to external accounting software.
</Note>
