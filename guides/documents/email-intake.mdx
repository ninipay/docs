---
title: "Route emailed documents"
description: "Document intake tells Nini which organization should receive documents you email to Nini's intake address."
---

Your email address files into at most one organization at a time.

<Tip>
**Example:** You work with two organizations. Check the routing choice before emailing a receipt so it is filed under the intended organization.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Email intake must be enabled for the destination organization, and you need a Bookkeeper, Controller or Admin role there. Membership in an organization alone does not route your email.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Settings, then click Document intake under You. If it is absent, ask an administrator about availability and access.
2. Read File my emailed documents under. Don't route my email means your emailed documents are not filed into any organization.
3. Choose the intended organization. The change saves straight away and a confirmation names the organization. Only organizations where you can enroll are listed.
4. Find the intake address: open Documents, click Upload and read the Or email files to line at the bottom of the dialog. Click it to copy the address. Do not use an address copied from this guide.
5. For an agreed first test, send one suitable sample from your own email address to that intake address.
6. Open Documents in the destination organization and find the sample. Open it to confirm the organization and file are correct; the panel shows which address sent it, and the Email tab shows the message it came in.

<Check>
**Expected result:** The sample arrives under the selected organization and can be inspected.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If it does not arrive, check the sending address, your routing choice and whether email intake is enabled for the organization before resending.
</Warning>

## Manage who can email documents in

Administrators can click Settings, then Inbound senders under Organization. There you can turn email intake on or off for the organization, see everyone whose emailed documents file into it, and revoke a sender. Members opt in themselves from Document intake. To accept documents from someone outside the organization, click Add external sender; they receive a verification email and their documents only start filing in once they confirm.
