---
title: "Review information extracted from a document"
description: "Nini can turn a document into separate transactions."
---

Reviewing means checking those transactions against the original so a reading mistake does not end up in your books.

<Tip>
**Example:** A bank statement has eight transaction lines. Check all eight, including whether a withdrawal has been read with the correct sign and date.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Find the file in Documents and click it. Have a readable original. Nini flags anything it is unsure it read correctly, but that does not replace your review.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Open the file from Documents. The original appears beside tabs for Review, Records, Details and History, plus Email when the file was emailed in.
2. Use Zoom in, Zoom out, Rotate and the page arrows if needed to read the original.
3. Start with Review, which appears when Nini has something for you to check. Each item shows what Nini read now and, where it has one, a suggestion and the reason. Click Accept to take the suggestion or Fix to correct the value yourself.
4. Open Records and compare every transaction with the original: date and year, description, amount, plus or minus sign, and currency. Mark each one Looks right, or Leave out for a line that should not go into your books. Use the + button to add a transaction Nini missed.
5. Open Details for the document date, party, totals and other fields Nini read, and History to see earlier events.
6. When everything is checked, click Add to my books, or Add all to my books (with the number of lines) when some are still marked To check. Confirm in the dialog. Lines you left out are not added.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You can explain whether each transaction agrees with the original, and the document shows Done once it is in your books.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If Add to my books is disabled, hover over it for the reason, such as a transaction that needs a date, an amount to check or a missing document date. Fix the value rather than leaving out a correct line just to continue.
</Warning>

## Statements Nini did not accept automatically

For a bank statement, a warning titled Why this statement was not validated automatically lists what did not add up, such as an unreadable currency, a statement period, a closing balance that does not match the lines, repeated lines or several accounts in one file. When Gia has looked into it, What Gia found explains the likely cause. Each issue also appears in Review, where Fix lets you set the currency, closing balance or period, or takes you to the lines to check.

<Note>
To change a document after adding it, click Make changes. To keep a file out of your books entirely, open More actions and choose Don't use this file; you can still find it under the Not used filter.
</Note>
