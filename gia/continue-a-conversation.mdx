---
title: "Continue a conversation while you work"
description: "Your recent chats help you return to an earlier investigation."
---

The Gia side panel lets you work with a conversation alongside another Nini page.

<Tip>
**Example:** Open a Match, click Ask Gia about this match, and ask a follow-up that names the issue you want explained.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Know which investigation you want to continue. If changing topics, start a new chat so the question is easy to distinguish from earlier work.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. In the sidebar, your five most recent chats are listed under Ask. Select the one you want, or select All chats to see every conversation, newest first. Press ⌘K on All chats to search them.
2. Read the earlier question and its date range before continuing. An older answer may describe an older data snapshot.
3. Write a follow-up in the Reply to Gia box when it concerns that investigation. Restate the period if it has changed.
4. From another page, use Toggle Gia to open the side panel. Pick one of the suggested questions for that page or write your own. On a Match, click Ask Gia about this match to start a chat about it. In Records, select rows and click Ask Gia in the selection bar to attach them to the chat. You can also click the plus button in the message box to search for and attach documents, transactions and counterparties. Gia only receives the context you attach or name; simply opening a page does not supply all of it.
5. Each side-panel chat opens as a tab, so you can keep several investigations going. Use Move to Ask to continue a side-panel chat on the full Ask page, or Open in side panel on the Ask page to take a conversation with you.
6. Use New chat when starting a separate topic. Use Toggle Gia again to close the panel and give the underlying page more room.

## Organize your chats

On All chats, open the actions menu beside a conversation to rename or archive it. Switch to Archived to find archived chats and restore them. An archived conversation is read-only until you restore it.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You can return to an earlier question or start a separate investigation without losing the page you are reviewing.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If an old conversation appears to disagree with today's screen, check its original scope and ask for a current review using the same dates and sources. A chat marked "No answer yet" did not finish; open it to retry.
</Warning>

<Note>
A dot on Toggle Gia means Gia has sent you a new message. Click Toggle Gia to open it.
</Note>
