---
title: "Understand Books and entries waiting to post"
description: "Books shows your books of record, the accounting source treated as the reference for your organization, and how many entries are in Nini's ledger or waiting to go in."
---

Use it to see where your numbers live and what still needs approval before it reaches Nini's ledger.

<Tip>
**Example:** You approved a Match in Nini but cannot find a new entry in QuickBooks. Books explains that Nini keeps its own copy of entries and does not change anything in the connected system.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Know which accounting provider your organization uses. QuickBooks Desktop and QuickBooks Online are different connections and need different setup or recovery instructions.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Books in the sidebar.
2. Read the card that names your main set of books. Check its status (Connected, or Setup isn't finished), when it was last checked and the currency it reports in. Click Check now to refresh it, or open the more menu for Finish setup or Connection details.
3. Read the count tiles. In your books counts entries already in Nini's ledger; Waiting to go in counts Matches that still need approval. Waiting for a closed month appears only when a month is closed and some Matches fall inside it.
4. Read the note under the tiles. Nini keeps its own copy of these entries; nothing is written back to the accounting system.
5. To act on waiting entries, click Waiting to go in or the Approve entries button in the page header. Both open Records → Matches, where you review and approve each Match.
6. Use the month-close checklist to see what still blocks closing a month, such as bank lines not yet in your books or documents not yet checked. Each row links to the place where you can fix it.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You know which accounting source Nini treats as your books and how many entries are waiting to go into Nini's ledger.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If Books says no books of record is set, matching has nothing to treat as the source of truth. Pick one with Make books of record, or ask your accounting administrator. Do not add another connection simply to make the screen look right.
</Warning>

<Note>
Other connected accounting systems appear below the main card as read-only mirrors: Nini reads them but posts nothing there. If you have no accounting connection, Books can keep your books in Nini from the bank statements you upload.
</Note>
