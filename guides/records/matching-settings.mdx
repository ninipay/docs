---
title: "Read matching settings"
description: "Matching settings control how sure Nini must be before it suggests a Match, and whether it may approve Matches on its own."
---

These settings affect every Match in your organization, not only the item you are viewing.

<Tip>
**Example:** A wider date or amount tolerance suggests more possible Matches and leaves more for you to check.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Use this section to understand the current setup. Only administrators can change these settings, and an accounting administrator should decide changes under your organization's review policy.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Settings, then click Matching under Organization.
2. Read Automatic approval. When Approve matches automatically is on, Nini adds Matches to your books without waiting for you if it is at least as sure as How sure Nini must be. Most per minute caps how many it approves as a safety limit. Click How it works for details.
3. If you see Paused: too many matches went in at once, Nini has stopped approving automatically. An administrator can click Turn back on after checking what happened.
4. Open Advanced: how closely lines must agree to be matched. How sure Nini must be to suggest a match sets which suggestions are proposed; How far apart the dates can be sets three day tiers, tightest first; How far apart the amounts can be allows a difference in cents or a percentage, whichever is larger.
5. Read the units beside each value. Cents, percent and days measure different things. Administrators see Reset beside any value that differs from the default.
6. If you are an administrator and change a value, review the summary in the bar at the bottom of the page, then Save or Discard.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You can describe the current matching and automatic approval controls.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If you cannot change a value, you may not be an administrator. Ask an administrator before changing automatic approval: Matches approved this way go straight into your books and are kept in the audit log.
</Warning>

<Note>
Closed months are not set here. Matches dated in a closed month appear under In a closed month on Records → Matches, and Books shows how many are waiting for a closed month.
</Note>
