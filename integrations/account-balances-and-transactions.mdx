---
title: "View account balances and transactions"
description: "Accounts shows bank accounts and their activity."
---

Use it to locate money coming in or going out, confirm which account it belongs to and check how current the information is.

<Tip>
**Example:** A supplier says it was paid last week. Open the relevant bank account, go to its transactions for that date range, then compare the payment's date, amount and description.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Know the bank account and approximate date you want to check. A positive or negative sign describes the direction of an amount; compare it with the source.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Accounts in the sidebar. Each row shows the account name, type, masked number, balance, available balance and Status. Use Search accounts to find one, then click it.
2. Read the line under the account name: the connection status, when it was last updated and how many transactions Nini holds. If the status shows a problem, click Why? for the reason.
3. Read the tiles. Bank balance shows the balance with its as-of date; Unknown means the bank has not reported one, not that the balance is zero. To look at totals the transactions still waiting to be matched. The month-so-far tile shows money in and out, or Unknown when Nini has not heard from the bank recently.
4. Use Dates to set a period for the cash flow chart.
5. To check individual transactions, click See all transactions. Records opens filtered to this account; narrow it by date and compare each item's description, date, amount, currency and direction with the bank source.
6. If the account has transactions waiting, click Look at to open just those in Records.
7. To ask the bank for anything new straight away, click Check for new transactions.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You can identify the account, period and source of the payment you are checking.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If a transaction is missing, check the dates and filters in Records, then the account's status and last update. Banks send updates when there is something new, so a quiet account may simply have had no activity. Ask about import coverage if it exists at the bank but still does not appear.
</Warning>

<Note>
⋯ → Export CSV produces a file of this account's transactions for use outside Nini. ⋯ → Account settings shows when the account was connected, its currency and the range of transactions Nini holds.
</Note>
