title: "Ask a clear question"
description: "Gia is Nini's assistant for investigating information."
---

Use Ask to frame a question, read the answer and open the evidence behind it. A useful answer should help you decide where to look next.

<Tip>
**Example:** Ask which Matches need attention this month, then check a cited record and the Matches still waiting on review.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Choose a specific date range and topic. A citation is a link to supporting information; one citation supports its example, not every claim in an answer.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Click Ask in the sidebar and place the cursor in the Work with Gia message box. You can also use the microphone button to dictate your question.
2. Write one question. Include the period, the account or counterparty and the decision you are trying to make. For example: "For August 2026, which Matches need attention? List the reasons and link the supporting records."
3. Add "State the sources and date range you used, and tell me what is missing." If you are investigating only, add "Do not change any records."
4. Press Enter or click Send message. Gia shows what it is doing while it works, such as searching your records or reading the general ledger. To end the response early, click Stop generating; you can keep the partial answer or regenerate it.
5. Read the answer's scope before its conclusion. Check dates, currency, accounts and whether the response covers the full request or only examples. When Gia lays out transactions beside the answer, those rows are the actual records it used; select one to open it.
6. Use a follow-up in the same conversation, in the Reply to Gia box, to clarify a specific point, such as "Which evidence is missing from the first Match?" Follow [Check an answer against its evidence](/gia/check-evidence) before relying on the answer.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You have a response to a specific question and a clear next source or record to inspect.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If the answer is vague, ask for exact dates and sources. If it conflicts with the live queue, investigate the conflict instead of treating the answer as confirmation. If you leave the page before Gia finishes, the answer keeps being prepared and appears when you reopen the conversation.
</Warning>

<Note>
Do not assume that a conversational statement means Nini performed a posting, approval or other change. Gia changes records only through an action card you confirm or a proposal that is approved.
</Note>
