---
title: "Review your work from start to finish"
description: "A review session is complete when you know what arrived, what was checked and what still needs attention."
---

This sequence brings the different areas together without treating any one status or summary as proof that everything is finished.

<Tip>
**Example:** For a monthly supplier review, first confirm the feeds are current, check uploaded invoices, inspect the relevant Matches and list any missing evidence or delivery issues.
</Tip>

<Info>
**Before you start:** Choose one organization, a date range and the accounts or suppliers in scope. Keep that scope consistent as you move between screens.
</Info>

## Step by step

1. Check sources. Open Integrations and Accounts, read sync freshness and note any source that has not supplied the expected data. A dot beside Integrations or Settings in the sidebar means a connection needs you.
2. Check documents. Open Documents, choose For your books or For Gia to read, and look for statuses such as Needs a check, Reading or Couldn’t read. Open the original files behind items you need to review.
3. Check individual records. Open Records, stay on the Records tab and choose To review. Inspect what each line needs and confirm that the relevant dates and sources are included. Use To approve for matches waiting on you.
4. Check grouped evidence. Switch to the Matches tab, inspect the Needs you section and open each item. Review evidence, approval and ledger delivery separately.
5. Check related work. Open the Sets tab in Records if the question concerns a project, supplier or period containing several events. Inspect the members rather than relying on the group name or total alone.
6. Check ledger delivery. Use the Match's delivery information and Books as relevant. Record any pending or failed delivery separately from evidence issues.
7. Open Questions to confirm, correct or dismiss anything Gia asked about your statements. Then use Ask to have Gia summarize the remaining questions, with sources and the same scope. Verify the summary against the live queues.
8. Finish with a short review note: scope reviewed, sources current or missing, items resolved, items still open and the person who will handle each next step. Use your team's usual place to record it.

<Check>
**Expected result:** You can explain the review's coverage and hand off specific open items instead of saying only that the dashboard looks clear.
</Check>

<Warning>
**If you get stuck:** If counts differ, compare what each screen counts and its filters. A document, record, Match and Set are different units. An empty To review view does not mean nothing needs attention.
</Warning>

## Example review note

- Scope: operating account, supplier payments, August 2026.
- Sources: bank feed current; one supplier invoice still missing.
- Open work: one Match needs the invoice; one delivery status needs administrator review.
- Next step: request the missing invoice through the team's normal process and return to the two identified items.
